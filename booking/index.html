Good to know
Who can come?

Each booking is for 1–2 people: couples, friends, parent and child, or just you. Wedding band sessions are for 2.

Gold, platinum or silver?

Gold comes in 10K, 14K or 18K (yellow, white or pink), plus Pt950 platinum, in 1.0–5mm widths (1.0mm is 10K only). Sterling silver is a flat price per ring, from 1.5mm to 10mm, plus signet styles. Undecided? Book the session where you choose your metal with an artisan.

Can I engrave a drawing?

Yes. Our "ring tattoo" lets you engrave handwriting, a doodle or even your pet onto your ring.

Need a different start time?

Start times can be flexible. Text us at (646) 957-0489.

Want something one of a kind?

Book a Design Ring Consultation. Design it with our artisans in NY, and our master jewelers in Japan bring it to life.
