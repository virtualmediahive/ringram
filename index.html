Quick note

Karat vs. carat

They sound the same, but they're different. Karat (K) measures gold purity: 10K, 14K or 18K at ringram. Carat (ct) measures a gemstone's weight. Birthstones added at ringram are small accent stones set into the band. Our artisans will show you the options in person.
